Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This policy explains what information INVELSOFT ("we", "us") collects through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using this website or submitting the request form, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

When you submit the request form, we collect the information you enter:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address
  • The type of issue you selected and the device you use (Windows, Mac, Android or iPhone / iPad)

If you call or email us, we also receive the information you share with us during that contact.

How we use your information

We use your information to contact you about your request by phone or email, to understand and help with your issue, and to keep a record of our communication with you. We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

Your request is stored in our customer relationship management (CRM) system, provided by Zoho Corporation, which processes it on our behalf. We may also share information when the law requires it. We do not share your information with others for their own marketing.

Cookies and third-party services

This website does not set cookies of its own and does not use advertising or analytics trackers. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts, so those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser type when you visit.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as we need it to handle your request and for our business records, and then delete or anonymize it.

Your choices

You can ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, and you can ask us to stop contacting you. Email us at the address below and we will respond. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local privacy laws.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed.
